# 作文万能模板句

## 开头句式

**表达观点**
> As far as I am concerned, I agree with the latter opinion to some extent.

**引出话题**
> When it comes to playing computer games, he will be very excited.

**众所周知**
> It is universally acknowledged that trees are indispensable to us.
> As is known to all, ...

## 论证句式

**强调重要性**
> We cannot emphasize the importance of protecting our eyes too much.
> （再怎么强调...的重要性也不为过）

**不可否认**
> There is no denying that...

**绝对不能**
> On no account can we ignore the value of knowledge.

## 结尾句式

**总结**
> Therefore, as stated above, ...
> In conclusion, ...

**原因**
> That is the reason why I don't like...
> The reason is that...

| 突然想到 | It occurred to me that... |
